Training Session

The Art of
Noticing

Empowering curiosity and understanding in merchant environments.

Objectives

Session Goals

Identify Friction

Recognize visual and verbal cues of environmental hurdles in any merchant setup.

Engage with Ease

Framework for low-stakes, curiosity-driven dialogue with business owners.

Bridge to Support

Empowerment to offer help without the weight of technical expertise.

Alignment

Mission Driven

The Mission

Supporting the small business community.

The Support

Turning curiosity into conversations that improve situations.

Activity

The Expertise Barrier

What industry knowledge do you feel you must have before helping a customer?

Chat Reflection

In the chat, list the specific details (pricing, hardware, rates) you feel stop you from speaking up.

Mindset

Natural Sharing

Sharing a solution that works is not a pitch—it is an act of service.

When you see someone struggling with a problem that you know how to solve, the instinct to help is natural.

The Foundation
"It’s not about what you know,
it’s about what you notice."

Observation

Identifying visual friction.

Listening

Hearing the emotional tone.

Lesson 01

Tone Reveals the Problem

Listen for the "Stress Shift."

Hardware pride often hides cost anxiety. Their tone will tell you they are oversold long before you see a data sheet.

Right-sizing begins with hearing the mismatch.

Lesson 02

Empathy for Friction

Bottlenecks are visible to everyone.

When a process stresses staff, it creates visible friction. If it looks like a pain in the neck, it probably is.

Empathy for their reality is the bridge.

Lesson 03

Feedback as a Symptom

Complaints indicate desire for change.

Hearing a third-party complaint is a clear indicator of a gap. You don't need the math to recognize a service failure.

Discovery starts with frustration.

Observation

Environmental Indicators

Manual Entry

Hand-typing card numbers into systems during checkout.

Disjointed Systems

Multiple disconnected screens or tablets on the counter.

Underutilized Tech

High-cost systems being used only for basic functions.

Fee Signage

Notes about extra fees or card minimums.

Customer Feedback

Dissatisfaction overheard in the environment.

Unique Setups

Opportunity to learn from niche operational flows.

Dialogue

Starting the Conversation

Curiosity

"How does a shop like yours typically handle payments?"

Empathy

"I noticed you're keying those in manually—does that normally happen?"

Impact

"If you could change one thing about your checkout, what would it be?"

Context

"Who do you use for processing? I'm curious what shops here prefer."

The Connection

"I work for Gravity. We focus on improving payment environments. If you're open to it, I'd love to see if we can help improve your situation."

If they say no, the relationship is still intact.

You offered help, and they are okay.
