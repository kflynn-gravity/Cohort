Situational Awareness

Identifying the actual friction points where value can be added.

Challenge: The Mismatched Solution

Scenario

A small shop uses a massive, complex POS system. The owner is proud but stressed about the price and only uses the basic functions.

What does the tone reveal?

Key Point

Tone reveals the problem long before data does. Mismatched solutions create a visible weight.

Did you need to know the price or the software specs to see the stress?

Challenge: Visible Friction

Scenario

A busy coffee shop with a massive line. The worker is manually typing in 16-digit card numbers for every customer while cars wait.

Where is the friction here?

Key Point

If a process looks like a "pain in the neck," it probably is. Expertise isn't required to spot a bottleneck.

Did you need to know interchange rates to see a frustrated employee?

Challenge: The Feedback Loop

Scenario

A customer complains about a processing fee on their city utility bill. You don't know the exact rates or what is considered "high."

What is the indicator here?

Key Point

A complaint is a symptom. You don't need to do the math to know there is a problem to solve.

Did you need to know the utility budget to recognize a dissatisfied citizen?

Listening > Industry Knowledge

Look back at the list of things you felt you "needed" to know at the start of this session.

In every scenario we just covered, not a single one required technical industry knowledge.

They required:

  • • Recognizing a need or a pain point.
  • • Listening to the merchant's perspective.
  • • Having the curiosity to ask, "Does it always work this way?"

Understanding what to look for

Manual Processes

Hand-typing card numbers into systems.

Cluttered Environments

Multiple screens that don't communicate.

Underused Solutions

High-cost systems used for only basic functions (Oversold).

Customer Feedback

Hearing complaints about fees or checkout difficulty.

Operational Friction

Troubleshooting tech instead of leading.

Unique Setups

An opportunity to learn from the merchant.

Conversational Foundations

Curiosity

"How does a shop like yours typically handle payments?"

Empathy

"I noticed you're keying that in—does that normally happen?"

Impact

"If you could change one thing about your checkout, what would it be?"

Context

"Who do you use for processing? I'm curious what shops here prefer."

Activity: Pick one question and share it with a partner. Why does this approach resonate?

Building the Bridge

"I work for Gravity. We focus on improving payment environments. If you're open to it, I'd love to see if we can help improve your situation."

If they say no, the relationship is still intact. You offered help, and they are okay.
