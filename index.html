Investigation

Spotting Pain Points

Inefficient Time

Manual entry, double-typing, or broken hardware. If it's slow for them, it's slow for the customer.

Visible Frustration

Signs about "Fees" or "Card Minimums" are usually signs of a merchant who feels trapped by their current setup.

Case Study

The High-Cost Pride

A shop owner has a $5,000 system. He's proud of the hardware, but his tone changes when talking about the monthly costs.

The Pain Point: They are Oversold. They have more tech than they need and it's draining their profit. We can help right-size them.

Strategic Shift

The Happy Merchant

A Happy Merchant is an Advocate

If they love their setup, they are your best source for a referral. They likely know other business owners who are struggling.

The "Bridge" Question:

"I love to hear that! We see a lot of people struggling with [Inefficiency]. Since you're in a good spot, do you know any other business owners who are frustrated with their setup?"

Mindset

Natural Sharing

Sharing a solution is an act of service. If you see a neighbor struggling with a heavy door, you hold it open. This is no different.

"I work for Gravity. We focus on fixing those kinds of inefficiencies. If you're open to it, I'd love to connect you with an expert to see if we can help."
