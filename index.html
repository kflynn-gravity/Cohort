YOUR ZONE: IDENTIFYING INEFFICIENCIES

The Investigation

Spotting the Problems

We are looking for plain-language inefficiencies, not technical data.

Time-Consuming Tasks

Does the checkout process look slow or redundant?

Tech Frustration

Does the staff look annoyed by their equipment?

Story 01

The "Ferrari" Setup

A small shop with a $5,000 system. The owner is proud, but their tone changes when they talk about the monthly bill.

The Problem: They are oversold. They are paying for a Ferrari to drive a block. You don't need to know the math to hear "Stress."
Story 02

The Manual Bottleneck

A busy line where every single card number is being typed in by hand. The staff is sweating; the customers are waiting.

The Inefficiency: It is Time-Consuming. If it looks like a pain in the neck, it is.
Dialogue

Simple Mode

If you don't like "selling" or aren't sure what you're seeing, use these fallback questions:

"Hey, is that easy to use?"

"That looks time-consuming, is that how you always do that?"

"Does that happen a lot?" (The Pattern Question)

The Best Case Scenario

What if they are "Good to Go"?

If they love their setup... say "COOL!"

Celebrate their success. You haven't failed; you've confirmed your community is thriving. That is mission-success.

Mindset Shift

Natural Sharing

Sharing a solution is not a pitch—it is an act of service. If you see a neighbor struggling and you have the "jack" to lift their car, you share it.

"I work for Gravity. We fix those kinds of inefficiencies. If you're open to it, I'd love to connect you with an expert."
