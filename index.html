Investigation

Observing Inefficiencies

The Time-Sink

Manual entry or broken hardware. If it's slow for them, it's slow for you.

The Human Toll

Staff frustration, stress, or "apologizing for the system." Look for the eye-roll.

Fragmented Flow

Systems that don't talk. Paper trails, sticky notes, or broken hand-offs.

Scenario 2

The Manual Bottleneck

A coffee shop staff is manually keying in card numbers because the reader is broken. The line is out the door and staff is flustered.

The Observation: Manually keying cards even with a card reader

The Curiosity Question: "Is that annoying to have to type it in?"

Scenario 3

The Fee Friction

A City Utility office has high convenience fees for online payments. Because of this, customers swarm the lobby to pay in person.

The Observation: A customer complaint

The Curiosity Question: "Are you getting a lot of complaints?"
