The "Why" Behind the Training

Gravity’s mission is to support the small business community. To do that, we have to be able to talk to them.

Empowerment over Expertise

For a long time, I questioned what I could even say to a merchant. It was only through training that I felt empowered to realize: I don't need to be an expert to have a conversation.

This session is about giving you that same empowerment. It isn’t about selling; it’s about realizing you have the permission to be curious.

The "Natural Sharing" Concept

Think of a service, tool, or local business you genuinely believe in because it solves a problem or improves a situation.

"If you saw someone struggling with the exact problem this thing solves, would you share it with them?"

This instinct is at the heart of our work. Sharing the potential to make a situation better isn't a "pitch"—it's an act of service.

Understanding & Striving

We aren't here to make guaranteed claims. Our mission is to understand a merchant's environment and strive to see if there is a way to improve it.

Curiosity First

Lesson: Tone Reveals the Problem

You don't need a statement or a data sheet to identify a mismatched solution.

  • ✓ Listen for the "Stress Shift"
  • ✓ Identify over-utilization vs. over-cost

Key Takeaway

"How does a shop like yours typically handle payments?"

Let the merchant teach you. Their tone will tell you if they're happy or just 'over-selled.'

☕

Lesson: Empathy for Friction

Expertise isn't required to see a bottleneck. If a process looks like a "pain in the neck," it likely is.

The Lead-in

"I noticed you're keying those in manually—does that normally happen?"

The Human Factor

A merchant's environment affects their employees and their customers. Start where the friction is most visible.

Understanding their perspective and feelings will tell you everything you need to know.

Understanding what to look for

Manual Processes

Hand-typing card numbers into systems. Slows business and creates errors.

Cluttered Environments

Multiple tablets or screens on the counter that don't communicate.

Underused Solutions

High-cost systems used for only basic functions. A sign of being over-sold.

Direct Pain Points

Taped-up signs about card minimums or extra fees to cover processing costs.

Operational Friction

Owners troubleshooting technology instead of running their shop.

Unique Setups

Seeing a setup you haven't seen before is a chance to learn from the merchant.

Questions to start conversations

The Curiosity Question

"How does a shop like yours typically handle payments?"

The Empathy Question

"I noticed you're keying that in—does that normally happen?"

The Improvement Question

"If you could change one thing about your checkout, what would it be?"

The Learning Question

"Who do you use for processing? I'm curious what shops here prefer."

Making the Connection

A Sincere Approach:

"I work for Gravity. We focus on improving payment environments. If you're open to it, I'd love to see if we can help improve your situation."

If they say no, the relationship is still intact. You offered help, and they are okay.
