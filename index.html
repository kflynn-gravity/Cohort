The Insight Series

Three Stories

Story 01

The Board Game Shop

A owner with a massive $5,000 system. He's proud, but stressed about the cost and only uses 10% of the features.

The Stress Shift: Hardware pride often hides cost anxiety. You didn't need a data sheet to hear he was oversold.

Story 02

The Coffee Shop

12 cars in line. A barista is sweating because she has to manually type in a 16-digit card number for every customer.

Friction is Visible: If it looks like a pain in the neck, it probably is. Expertise isn't required to spot a bottleneck.

Story 03

The City Utility Bill

Overhearing a citizen complain about a processing fee at City Hall. You don't know the exact rate, but you hear the gap.

Symptoms Matter: A complaint is an indicator of a gap. You don't need the math to recognize a desire for change.

The Best Case Scenario

What if they love it?

If a merchant says their setup is perfect, Celebrate with them.

We aren't looking to "snake" merchants away from good situations. If they are happy, learn from them.

"That's awesome! I love hearing that. What's the name of that software? I'd love to learn what they're doing right."

The Outcome

You’ve built a relationship, learned about a competitor's strength, and kept the mission of support first.

The Hand-off

How to Refer

  • 1
    Basic Info: Just Name, Business Name, and Phone Number.
  • 2
    Observations: Write what you noticed (e.g., "Manual entry bottleneck").
  • 3
    The Hand-off: Hit submit. An expert takes it from there.
