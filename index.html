Framework

The Three Phases of Engagement

Phase 1: Identify

Determine where you want to sit. Who in your community do you want to help?

Phase 2: Engage

Have the easy, simple, comfortable conversation. Just curiosity.

Phase 3: Refer

If they're open to it, build the bridge to the experts at Gravity.

Investigation

What to Look For

The Time-Sink

Manual entry or broken hardware. If it's slow for them, it's slow for you.

The Human Toll

Staff frustration or "apologizing for the system." Look for the eye-roll.

Fragmented Flow

Systems that don't talk. Paper trails or sticky notes on screens.

Workshop

Live Practice: The Curiosity Pattern

Let's take a real scenario from the group. What have you noticed lately?

Scenario 1

The "Ferrari" Setup

A small board game shop has a massive $5,000 system. He's proud of the tech, but mentions the monthly overhead is "rough."

The Challenge: They are Oversold. "Whoa, that looks fancy! Does it actually make your life easier for what it costs?"
Scenario 2

The Manual Bottleneck

Barista manually keys in numbers because the reader is broken. The line is out the door.

The Challenge: Friction. "I noticed you're having to type those in—that looks like a hassle. Does that happen a lot?"
