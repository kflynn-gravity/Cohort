The Art of Noticing

Empowering curiosity and understanding in merchant environments

Session Objectives

Identify Friction

Equip participants with tools to recognize environmental hurdles in a merchant's setup.

Engage with Ease

Introduce a framework for low-stakes, curiosity-driven dialogue with business owners.

Bridge to Support

Foster empowerment to offer genuine help without the weight of industry expertise.

The "Why" Behind the Training

Empowerment over Expertise

Gravity’s mission is to support the small business community. This stems from a personal journey: questioning what to say for years, then feeling empowered once the focus shifted to simple conversations.

We are here to help, and that starts with the permission to be curious.

Skills Needed to Help

Observation

Identify visual and verbal cues of friction or dissatisfaction in the environment.

Listening

Understand the merchant’s perspective and emotional tone regarding their setup.

"It’s not about what you know, it’s about what you notice."

Lesson 1: Tone Reveals

Insight: Listen for the "Stress Shift."

A merchant may take pride in their hardware but feel the weight of its cost or complexity. Their tone will tell you they are oversold long before you see a single data sheet.

Right-sizing begins with hearing the mismatch.

Lesson 2: Friction Empathy

Insight: Expertise is not required to see a bottleneck.

When a process slows down business and stresses staff, it creates visible friction. If it looks like a pain in the neck, it probably is.

Empathy for the worker's reality is your bridge to a conversation.

Lesson 3: Symptoms

Insight: Complaints indicate a desire for change.

Hearing a third-party complaint (like a citizen talking about utility fees) is a clear indicator that something is wrong. You don't need to do the math to recognize a service gap.

Discovery starts by acknowledging the frustration.

Environmental Indicators

Manual Entry

Hand-typing card numbers into systems.

Disjointed Systems

Multiple disconnected screens on the counter.

Underutilized Tech

High-cost systems used for basic functions.

Fee Signage

Handwritten notes about fees or minimums.

Customer Feedback

Direct complaints heard in the environment.

Unique Setups

Setups you haven't seen—a chance to learn.

Questions to Start Conversations

  • Curiosity: "How does a shop like yours typically handle payments?"
  • Empathy: "I noticed you're keying those in manually—does that normally happen?"
  • Impact: "If you could change one thing about your checkout, what would it be?"
  • Context: "Who do you use for processing? I'm curious what shops here prefer."

Bridge to Support

"I work for Gravity. We focus on improving payment environments. If you're open to it, I'd love to see if we can help improve your situation."

If they say no, the relationship is still intact. You offered help, and they are okay.
