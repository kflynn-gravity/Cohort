Investigation

Observing Inefficiencies

Operational Bottlenecks

Things that look slow, repetitive, or difficult for the staff to manage.

Cost Inefficiency

Signs like "Cash Only" or "3% Fee" usually point to a merchant frustrated by their overhead.

Scenario 1

The "Ferrari" Setup

A small board game shop has a massive $5,000 system. He's proud of the hardware, but his tone shifts when talking about monthly overhead.

The Challenge: They are Oversold. They are paying for complexity they don't need. It is a drain on their dream.
Scenario 2

The Manual Bottleneck

A coffee shop staff is manually keying in card numbers because the reader is broken. The line is out the door.

The Challenge: Friction. Their tools are fighting them. This bottleneck is a physical barrier to their success.
Scenario 3

The Fee Friction

A City Utility office has high convenience fees for online payments. Because of this, customers choose to pay in person.

The Challenge: Operational Burden. High fees are driving manual work. The staff is overwhelmed by in-person traffic because the digital solution is too expensive for the neighbor.

Reinforcement

Natural Sharing

Sharing a solution is an act of service. If you notice a neighbor with a flat tire, you offer a jack. You don't "pitch" them a jack.

"I work for Gravity. We focus on fixing those kinds of inefficiencies. If you're open to it, I'd love to connect you with someone here who can help."
