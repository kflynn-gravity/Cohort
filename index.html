Perspective

Layers of Community

We use the word "Community" a lot. But community isn't a single flat line; it's a set of relationships.

  • Outer Circle: Businesses you walk past but haven't entered yet.
  • Middle Circle: The local lunch spot or gym you visit monthly.
  • Inner Circle: Places you've visited for 10+ years.
  • Center: Your best friends and family who own businesses.

Mindset

Breaking the Barrier

We want to get you from a 2 to a 6, or a 6 to a 10. Let's address what holds the scale back:

"I don't want to be salesy."
Curiosity isn't a pitch. It's a neighborly question.
"I don't have technical answers."
You don't need them. You are the bridge to our experts.
"I don't want to offend."
Observing a struggle is empathy, not judgment.
"They seem too busy."
A 10-second observation is fast and respectful.
Investigation

What to Notice

The Time-Sink

Manual entry or broken hardware. If it's slow for them, it's slow for you.

The Human Toll

Staff frustration or "apologizing for the system." Look for the eye-roll.

Fragmented Flow

Systems that don't talk. Sticky notes, paper trails, or broken hand-offs.
