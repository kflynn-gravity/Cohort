Live Practice: The Curiosity Pattern

Let's take a real scenario. Which "Touchpoint" category does this business fit?

The goal: Move the needle. If you're a 3/10 likelihood because you "don't know what to say," a simple 10-second question makes you an 8/10.

Scenario 1

The "Ferrari" Setup

A small board game shop has a massive $5,000 system. He's proud of the tech, but his tone changes when talking about monthly overhead.

The Observation: A fancy POS system

The Curiosity Question: "Wow, that looks fancy!"

Scenario 2

The Manual Bottleneck

A coffee shop staff is manually keying in card numbers because the reader is broken. The line is out the door.

The Observation: Manually keying cards even with a card reader

The Curiosity Question: "Is that annoying to have to type it in?"

Scenario 3

The Fee Friction

A City Utility office has high fees for online payments, so everyone swarms the lobby to pay in person.

The Observation: A customer complaint

The Curiosity Question: "Are you getting a lot of complaints?"
