Investigation

Visible Pain Points

Inefficient Time

Manual entry, double-typing, or broken hardware. If it looks slow, it's a problem.

Visible Frustration

Handwritten signs about "Card Minimums" or "3% Fees" mean they hate their setup.

Case Study

The High-Cost Pride

A shop owner has a $5,000 system. He's proud of the hardware, but his tone changes when talking about the monthly costs.

The Pain Point: They are Oversold. They are paying for a Ferrari to drive a block. A neighbor shouldn't be overcharged for their dream.
Simple Mode

"That looks time-consuming, is that how you always do that?"

Strategy

The Happy Merchant

Opportunity Still Exists

If a merchant is happy, they are your best advocate. They likely have friends who own businesses and AREN'T happy.

The Script: "I love to hear that! We see a lot of people struggling with [Inefficiency]. Since you're in a good spot, do you know any other business owners who are frustrated with their setup?"

Mindset

Natural Sharing

Sharing a solution is an act of service. If you see a neighbor struggling with a flat tire, you offer a jack. This is no different.

"I work for Gravity. We focus on fixing those kinds of inefficiencies. If you're open to it, I'd love to connect you with an expert to see if we can help."
