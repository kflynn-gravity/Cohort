Observation Challenge: The Mismatched Solution

The Scenario

A small, low-traffic shop uses a massive, complex POS system. The owner is proud but stressed about the price and only uses the basic 'swipe' function.

What does the tone reveal?

The Lesson

Tone reveals the problem long before the data does. Mismatched solutions feel like a weight.

"How does a shop like yours typically handle payments?"

Observation Challenge: Visible Friction

The Scenario

A busy coffee shop with a massive line. The worker has an iPad with a reader, but is manually typing in 16-digit card numbers for every customer.

Where is the friction here?

The Lesson

Empathy for friction is the bridge. If it looks like a "pain in the neck," it is.

"I noticed you're keying those in manually—does that normally happen?"

Understanding what to look for

Manual Processes

Hand-typing card numbers into systems.

Cluttered Environments

Multiple screens that don't talk to each other.

Underused Solutions

High-cost systems used only for basic functions. A sign of being oversold.

Direct Pain Points

Taped-up signs about minimums or fees.

Operational Friction

Owners troubleshooting tech instead of leading.

Unique Setups

A chance to learn how a specific niche works.

Questions to start conversations

Curiosity

"How does a shop like yours typically handle payments?"

Empathy

"I noticed you're keying that in—does that normally happen?"

Impact

"If you could change one thing about your checkout, what would it be?"

Market Context

"Who do you use for processing? I'm curious what shops here prefer."
